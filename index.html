AI in practice

How the work gets done here.

AI at Nerdy is a working system: a way to sharpen context, tighten feedback, and make decisions with better evidence. It is embedded in craft, review, measurement, and collaboration.

01 / Engineering

Build with evals, observability, and fast review loops.

Engineers use AI to explore designs, draft tests, review changes, and investigate production signals while keeping ownership of the systems they ship.

02 / Product

Turn product questions into prototypes and evidence.

PMs and designers use AI to synthesize learner signal, stress-test flows, and define evaluation criteria before experiences reach learners.

03 / Sales

Make every conversation more prepared and specific.

Go-to-market teams use AI to understand school and family context, tailor follow-ups, and connect the right learning solution to the right need.

04 / Operations

Keep decisions connected to live learner and team context.

Operations teams use AI to organize knowledge, surface patterns, and coordinate support so people can make clearer, faster decisions.

How we work

Editorial standards for a high-ownership team.

01

Start with the learner.

The clearest work begins with a learner problem, a signal we can observe, and a way to know whether the experience improved.

02

Own the loop.

Teams stay close to discovery, build, launch, support, and measurement so learning compounds instead of getting handed off.

03

Move with taste.

We value speed, but not noise. Strong craft, readable systems, and practical judgment are how fast work stays durable.

04

Make the work legible.

We write things down, show evidence, share context, and make it easy for the next person to improve what we shipped.
