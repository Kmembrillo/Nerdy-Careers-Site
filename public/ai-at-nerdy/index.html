A note from the team

AI at Nerdy

What AI-first actually means — for the engineers, product managers, and operators building here.

"AI-first" has become one of those phrases that means everything and nothing. Every company claims it. Most mean "we added a chatbot." Here's what it means at Nerdy.

WE BUILD WITH AI. CONSTANTLY.

Almost all software development at Nerdy is done with AI. That's not a talking point — it's a measurable operational reality. Our engineers pair with Claude Code daily, for architecture decisions, code reviews, refactors, and test generation. The question isn't whether to use AI on a given task. The question is which tool, at what level of autonomy, for which part of the problem.

This has changed the nature of what an engineer here does. Less time on boilerplate. More time on the hard parts — system design, product thinking, and the judgment calls that AI still can't make.

Callout From our Q1 2026 shareholder letter — "Not only is all software development done almost exclusively with AI, we are using it to do everything from automate our back-office workflows to handle inbound and outbound calls and interactions with customers."

AI IS THE PRODUCT — AND THE OPERATING SYSTEM

At Nerdy, AI plays two distinct roles. The first is product: our Live + AI platform pairs expert human tutors with real-time generative AI to deliver personalized learning at scale. The AI layer inside a session can answer a student's question in the moment, surface the right next concept, and flag when a learner is stuck — all in the context of their actual learning history.

The second role is operational. Every team at Nerdy — engineering, product, sales, operations, finance — uses AI to move faster and do more with less. Recruiters, lawyers, account managers, and analysts all have personal agent stacks.

AI is how we work. What we sell is the relationship between a learner and a great tutor — now supported by the best technology available.

MAYA: AI THAT FEELS LIKE A RELATIONSHIP

The most visible expression of our AI-first product approach is Maya, our AI concierge built into the learner experience. Maya is always on — she answers questions, surfaces the right next step, helps a student schedule a session, and resolves day-to-day issues without a phone call or support ticket.

Maya has full context on each student: their learning plan, past tutoring sessions, diagnostic results, and product interactions. For a student or parent, she turns a platform into something that feels alive, responsive, and genuinely useful.

Maya is not a FAQ bot. She is a persistent, personalized guide built on production LLMs, running across 36,900+ active learning members.

EVALS ARE THE NEW SPECS

One of the clearest signals that a team is genuinely AI-native is how they handle quality assurance for model-powered features. At Nerdy, every product change that touches a model goes through an eval suite before it reaches a learner.

Our product managers write evals, not just specs. When a PM scopes a new feature, they define what good looks like — and that definition becomes a test that runs in CI. This means model regressions get caught before they ship, not after.

HOW TEAMS ACTUALLY USE AI — DAY TO DAY

ENGINEERING

Engineers use Claude Code for the majority of their development work — first drafts of features, test generation, PR reviews, and postmortem synthesis. On-call is AI-augmented: agents triage alerts, identify likely root causes, and draft incident summaries. The result is a smaller team shipping more product.

PRODUCT

PMs write prompt-and-eval pairs as part of every feature spec. Discovery research uses AI to synthesize learner sessions at a scale that wasn't possible before. Roadmaps are continuous — decisions get made in days, not quarters.

SALES & GTM

Our sales reps run a team of one human and a fleet of agents. AI listens on district calls to draft tailored proposals. Pipeline forecasts are refreshed daily from CRM and email signals. Top reps spend most of their day talking to humans, not entering data.

OPERATIONS & SUPPORT

Maya handles a meaningful share of in-product customer interactions — answering questions, rescheduling sessions, resolving common issues. Human support focuses on the complex cases that require judgment.

HR & PEOPLE

At Nerdy, even HR ships code. Our People Team uses AI to build internal tools — offboarding systems, manager playbooks with embedded AI colleagues, and self-service portals — so they can spend their time on the calls that actually need a human.
